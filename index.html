Privacy Policy

Complete Koine Course, for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This is the policy shown inside the app.

This app collects no information about you. There are no accounts of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software in the app, and no server of ours that the app talks to.

Your progress — the lessons you have completed and your flashcard reviews — is stored on your device. If you use iCloud, Apple keeps that progress in step across your own Apple devices through your private iCloud database. We never see it.

The only connections the app makes are to Apple: the App Store, for purchase, restore and updates; iCloud, for that synchronisation within your own Apple account; and Apple's servers, to download the full course after purchase. We receive nothing from any of them.

There is nothing to delete or export on our side, because nothing is held on our side. Removing the app from your device removes its data from that device; your iCloud data is yours to manage in your Apple account.

If you write to us at the feedback address, we keep your message only for as long as it takes to reply, and we never add you to a list.
